# Spec QA: Chronology Hall Expansion

## Result

Approved.

## Checks

- The new scope stays bounded to the homepage workstream instead of triggering
  a full site redesign
- Only the required historically significant devices are mandated
- The Apple-style spotlight format is translated into museum language rather
  than product marketing
- The room structure stays chronological and legible

## Risks

- Minimal copy could become too shallow if it loses historical meaning
- A visual-first structure could drift away from Swiss restraint if the page
  becomes too decorative
- Innovation Era contains two devices, so the room must preserve one-device-at-a-time emphasis

## Guardrails

- keep each spotlight focused on one device
- use scarcity as preservation language
- keep the route explicit from Origins through Modern Era
